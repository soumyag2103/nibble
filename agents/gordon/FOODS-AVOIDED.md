# FOODS-AVOIDED.md

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Absolute Restrictions (medical/safety)
- Honey — botulism risk, never under 12 months
- Whole cow's milk as drink — under 12 months (dairy in cooking, yogurt, paneer: fine)
- Added salt — kidneys not ready
- Added sugar
- Whole nuts — choking hazard (thinned nut butter OK for allergen intro)
- Raw hard vegetables — choking hazard (steamed soft only)

## Family Dietary Preference
- No pork
