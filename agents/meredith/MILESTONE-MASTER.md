# MILESTONE-MASTER.md
Sources: WHO Motor Development Study, AAP Developmental Surveillance 2022

## 6 Months Window (Typical: 5–7 months)

### Gross Motor
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Rolls front to back and back to front | 4–6m | 7m |
| Sits with support | 5–6m | 7m |
| Begins to sit briefly without support | 6–7m | 8m |
| Bears weight on legs when held standing | 5–7m | 8m |

### Fine Motor
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Reaches for objects with both hands | 4–5m | 6m |
| Brings objects to mouth | 4–6m | 7m |
| Transfers objects hand to hand | 6–7m | 8m |
| Rakes objects with fingers | 6–7m | 8m |

### Communication & Language
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Laughs and giggles | 4–5m | 6m |
| Vocalizes in response to speech | 5–6m | 7m |
| Babbles with consonant sounds (ba, da, ma, ga) | 5–7m | 8m |
| Responds to own name | 5–7m | 9m |
| Turns toward familiar voice/sound | 4–6m | 7m |

### Social & Cognitive
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Recognizes familiar faces | 4–6m | 7m |
| Shows interest in mirror | 5–6m | 8m |
| Explores objects with curiosity | 5–6m | 7m |
| Responds to peek-a-boo | 7–9m | 10m |
| Begins stranger anxiety | 6–8m | Not a concern if later |

## 7–9 Months Window (Upcoming)

### Gross Motor
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Sits steadily without support | 7–8m | 9m |
| Crawls (any style) | 7–9m | 10m |
| Sits up from lying/crawl position | 7–8m | 9m |
| Pulls to stand | 8–10m | 12m |
| Cruises along furniture | 9–10m | 12m |

### Fine Motor
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Pincer grasp (thumb + index) | 8–10m | 12m |
| Bangs objects together | 7–8m | 9m |
| Drops and picks up deliberately | 7–9m | 10m |

### Communication & Language
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Combines syllables (baba, dada, mama — non-meaningful) | 7–8m | 9m |
| Imitates sounds and simple gestures | 8–9m | 10m |
| Responds to "no" | 8–9m | 10m |
| Points at objects of interest | 9–12m | 13m |

### Social & Cognitive
| Milestone | Typical Range | Discuss with Doctor If Not By |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Plays peek-a-boo | 7–9m | 10m |
| Object permanence (looks for hidden object) | 8–9m | 10m |
| Stranger anxiety peaks | 8–10m | — |
| Clear preference for primary caregivers | 6–8m | 9m |
